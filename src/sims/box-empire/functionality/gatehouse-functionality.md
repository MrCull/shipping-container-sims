# Gatehouse Functionality

The gatehouse controls truck access to the terminal.

## Gate Lanes

The current gatehouse has two independent lane controls:

- Export lane: allows export delivery trucks to enter.
- Import lane: allows import pickup trucks to enter and leave after pickup.

The player can open or close each lane from the gatehouse panel. The tutorial narrator can also open both lanes when prompted.

## In-Gate Behavior

All trucks enter through the in-gate.

An export truck may enter only when the export lane is open.

An import pickup truck may enter only when the import lane is open.

Trucks queue outside the gate if their lane is closed or another truck is being processed.

## Gate Processing

A truck spends 15 seconds being processed at the gate before it drives into the terminal.

Only one truck is processed at the in-gate at a time.

## Out-Gate Behavior

All trucks leave through the out-gate.

Import pickup trucks use the out-gate after receiving a container. They earn gate-out revenue when processing is complete.

Export delivery trucks also leave after their container has been removed.

The import lane controls whether out-gate processing can proceed for import pickup trucks.

## Barriers

The gate barriers open visually for nearby trucks and close after trucks clear the gate area.
