# Career and Completion Functionality

Box Empire currently has a completed tutorial flow and a short career-mode story preview. Full career mode is not currently playable.

## Tutorial Completion Screen

After the tutorial is completed, the player sees a completion screen showing:

- Total money balance
- Number of import containers processed
- Number of export containers loaded
- Time taken

The player can replay the tutorial or continue to the next story chapter.

## Career Story Preview

Continuing after the tutorial opens a short story sequence about inheriting the terminal and preparing to grow it into a stronger business.

The story is presented across several pages:

- The player receives the terminal as an inheritance.
- The terminal is described as old, quiet, and financially weak.
- The player is framed as the person who can rebuild the operation.
- Career mode is shown as coming soon.

## Current Career Limitation

The current career screen does not start a new playable level. It communicates that future career gameplay will include deeper progression, upgrades, traffic growth, and larger vessels.

The player can return to the start or play the tutorial again.
