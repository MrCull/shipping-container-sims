# Events, Audio, and Feedback Functionality

Box Empire gives the player operational feedback through events, sounds, floating text, and visual reactions.

## Event Feed

The event feed records recent happenings in the terminal. It shows the simulation time and a short message.

Current event categories include:

- Container placed or picked
- Vessel container lifted or placed
- Money earned or spent
- Vessel announced, arriving, arrived, departing, or departed
- Truck arrived or departed
- Gate opened or closed
- Tutorial completed
- Level up
- Job created or completed
- Equipment idle

## Sounds

Sound effects are attached to major operational events.

Examples include:

- Container placement sounds
- Money earned sounds
- Cost payment sound
- Vessel horn for arrival and departure
- Tutorial completion cheer
- Level-up sound

## Ambient Audio

Ambient terminal audio starts after the gatehouse becomes active for the first time.

## Floating Money Text

When money is earned or spent, floating text appears near the relevant place in the 3D scene.

Positive money feedback is shown as earned money. Negative money feedback is shown as spent money.

## Vessel Feedback

When a container is loaded onto the vessel, the vessel gives visible feedback to make the placement feel impactful.
